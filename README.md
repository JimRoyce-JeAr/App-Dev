# My Favorite Horror Movies
Horror movies are my favorite because they are exciting, suspenseful, and entertaining. I enjoy watching movies that feature scary characters, mysterious events, and unexpected twists.

## 1. IT (2017)

**IT** is a supernatural horror movie about a group of children who encounter a terrifying entity known as Pennywise the Dancing Clown. The story takes place in Derry, where mysterious disappearances and frightening events occur.

## 2. Texas Chainsaw Massacre (2022)

**The Texas Chainsaw Massacre** is a slasher horror movie about a group of friends who encounter a dangerous family during their trip to Texas. The movie is known for its suspenseful atmosphere and iconic horror villain, Leatherface.

## 3. Terrifier (2016)

**Terrifier** is a slasher horror movie featuring Art the Clown, a mysterious and frightening character who terrorizes people on Halloween night. The movie is known for its dark atmosphere and unique horror villain.
